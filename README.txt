VIVAAH — Indian Wedding Atelier website demo

FILES
- index.html: page structure and content
- style.css: responsive styling and visual design
- script.js: mobile navigation, testimonial slider, enquiry form behaviour

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep all three files in the same folder.
3. Open index.html in a browser. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and remote sample photos.

BEFORE SELLING / LAUNCHING
- Replace “Vivaah” with the planner's real brand name, logo, contact details and social links.
- Replace the demo email hello@vivaahatelier.example in script.js with their real business email.
- Connect the enquiry form to a real form backend (such as Formspree/Netlify Forms) if they want enquiries stored online.
- Replace sample photography with images the planner has permission to use.
- The photos currently load from Unsplash URLs and require an internet connection.
- This is a front-end demo; no enquiry is stored on a server.
